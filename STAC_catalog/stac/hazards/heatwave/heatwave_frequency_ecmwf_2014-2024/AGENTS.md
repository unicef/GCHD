## Overview

A heatwave can be defined as a period where local excess heat accumulates over a sequence of unusually hot days and nights. Heatwave frequency refers to the number of heatwaves per year.

- Layout: one global Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF (collection-level `data` asset).
- Data URL: https://unidatadapmclimatechange.blob.core.windows.net/public/gchd_cog/heatwave/heatwave_frequency_ecmwf_2014-2024.tif
- Period: 2014-01-01 – 2024-12-31
- License: other (https://github.com/unicef/GCHD)
