# AGENTS.md: Hazard exposure statistics

Guidance for AI agents and LLMs working with this catalog. Generated by STAC_catalog/gchd_stac/docs.py.

## Overview

Population and child exposure to each hazard per administrative unit (ADM2, with ADM1 and ADM0 roll-ups), from the GCHD exposure pipeline. Join to the boundaries on the unit codes.
