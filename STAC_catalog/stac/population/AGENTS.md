# AGENTS.md: Population

Guidance for AI agents and LLMs working with this catalog. Generated by STAC_catalog/gchd_stac/docs.py.

## Overview

WorldPop R2025A constrained population estimates for 2025 at ~100 m, the basis of the exposure analysis. Children under 18 and all ages, each as female, male and total, one item per country and a global VRT mosaic per collection.
