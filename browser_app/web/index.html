How to get a free API key
  1. Open Google AI Studio, API keys and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Accept the terms. AI Studio creates a project for you. Click Create API key, copy the key and paste it above.
  3. To keep it free, do not set up billing for that project: skip any "Set up billing" or "Upgrade" button. A project with no billing account stays on the free tier and cannot be charged. In AI Studio, the project's plan shows Free tier.

Limits. The free tier allows a limited number of requests per minute and per day, shown in AI Studio for your project. One question takes several requests. When the per-minute limit is reached, the app waits and carries on. When the daily limit is reached, try again the next day: it resets at midnight Pacific time.

Privacy. On the free tier, Google may use what the app sends (your questions and the summary numbers it reads) to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. GCHD data is public, but do not type personal or confidential information.

Google's terms: you must be 18 or over, and free-tier use is restricted in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. See the Gemini API terms and pricing.

Your key is kept only in this browser and is sent only to the provider you choose. GCHD has no server of its own, so it never sees your key or your questions.
