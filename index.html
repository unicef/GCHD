Methodology

How is children's exposure calculated?

A four-stage pipeline transforms raw hazard datasets into globally comparable children's exposure estimates at country and sub-national levels.

Stage 0 — Hazard Preparation

Individual hazard datasets are downloaded from authoritative global sources and uploaded to cloud data storage (Azure and GEE) as standardised raster assets.

Stage 1 — Multi-Hazard Indicators

Two composite indicators are derived: the Multi-Hazard Count (MHC), which counts the number of distinct hazard categories a location is exposed to, and the Multi-Hazard Intensity (MHI), a continuous 0–10 score capturing combined hazard severity using a PCA-based synthetic approach.

Stage 2 — Exposure Computation

Hazard layers are overlaid with WorldPop gridded children's population (2025) then aggregated into various administrative regions including country (Admin 0), regional (Admin 1) and district (Admin 2).

Stage 3 — Output & Dissemination

Exposure results are formatted and served through the interactive GCHD dashboard, UNICEF Data Warehouse, and UNICEF's GeoSight platform.

MHC

Multi-Hazard Count

Counts the number of distinct hazard categories a location is exposed to (max 8). Identifies hotspots where children face simultaneous hazards.

MHI

Multi-Hazard Intensity

Continuous 0–10 composite score using a PCA-based synthetic approach across 13 hazard layers. Distinguishes frequent mild events from rare catastrophic events.
